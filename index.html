About Me

I approach instructional design by identifying performance gaps, analyzing learner needs, and developing practical, engaging learning experiences that support measurable outcomes.

With a Master of Education in School Counseling and additional graduate training in professional counseling and education, I bring a strong foundation in learning theory, curriculum development, assessment design, and data-informed decision-making.

My work focuses on transforming complex information into accessible, interactive learning experiences. I incorporate instructional design principles, iterative development, and learner feedback to create purposeful solutions that support real-world performance.
